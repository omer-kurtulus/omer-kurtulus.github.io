In October 2026 Ömer Kurtuluş founded
[The Agora of New Music](https://theagoraofnewmusic.com), a free meeting place
for composers and performers of contemporary music. Performers find new works
to play and commission new pieces; composers find performers for their works.
There are no prizes, no juries and no entry fees.
