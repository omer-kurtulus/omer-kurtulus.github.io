Alicja Molitorys is compiling a catalogue of twenty-first-century flute
repertoire — extended techniques, approximate level of difficulty, and where the
score and any recording are accessible — published on her blog so that Polish
flutists can find work they would not otherwise come across. It is a reference
project rather than a performance one.

*Kakma-I*, for solo flute, was submitted to the call and is included.
