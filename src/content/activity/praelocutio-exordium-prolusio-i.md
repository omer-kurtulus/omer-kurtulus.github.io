*What the C\*rp\*s* has been selected for
*praelocutio.exordium.prolusio I.*, an international residency programme for
experimentalists, sound artists, makers and technologists produced by .abeceda
Institute.

The project will be developed through online seminars, experimentation, testing
and pre-production during 2026. The on-site phase takes place in Bled, Slovenia,
from 14–25 June 2027, with rehearsals and testing from 14–20 June followed by
realisation and public presentations from 21–25 June at the Seventh Bled
Contemporary Music Week.
