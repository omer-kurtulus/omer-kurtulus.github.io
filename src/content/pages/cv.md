## Artistic direction and leadership

**2021 – 2023** · Founder, Artistic Director and Instructor — **ÖKMA**, Istanbul, in
collaboration with Teknopol Istanbul / Istanbul Health Sciences University.
Founded and directed a composition-focused education programme for children and
young adults; taught composition to children from age five, including students on
the autism spectrum, as part of rehabilitation-oriented projects; led world-music
workshops for adults and conservatory students; founded and directed the Spektrogram
Vocal Ensemble.
