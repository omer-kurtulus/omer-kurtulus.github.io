Write to me about any of these — a short message is enough, and every message
is read:

- **Commissions** — new works for soloists, ensembles and orchestra, with or
  without electronics.
- **Scores and performance materials** — perusal scores, parts and technical
  riders for any work in the [catalogue](/works).
- **Recordings and multichannel masters** — multichannel and ambisonic works are
  available as full-resolution masters, and decodes can be prepared for a
  specific loudspeaker layout.
- **Collaborations** — live electronics and machine listening (Max/MSP,
  Somax2, FluCoMa), corpus-based work with performers, technical documentation.

If you are writing about a particular piece, its title in the subject helps —
every work page has a ready-made message for that.

## The Agora of New Music

I founded [The Agora of New Music](https://theagoraofnewmusic.com), a free
meeting place where performers find new works to play and composers find
performers — no prizes, no juries, no entry fees. For anything about the Agora,
write to [info@theagoraofnewmusic.com](mailto:info@theagoraofnewmusic.com).
