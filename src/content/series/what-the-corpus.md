*What the C\*rp\*s* is an ongoing project rather than a finished album. Each work
takes a corpus of recordings made by other people and a live exciter played by
the composer, and lets Somax2 — IRCAM's co-creative machine-listening system —
navigate and recombine the corpus in real time. The system never reproduces the
original sequence; fragments lose their identity and become part of an extended
electroacoustic morphology held together by repetition, deviation and continuous
renewal.

The question the project keeps returning to is the one its title puts: whose
music is this, when the material was recorded by other people, the sequence was
chosen by a machine listening in real time, and the composer's part is the
exciter, the trajectory and the intervention.

## Where the corpora come from

The three works so far are built from Creative Commons recordings published on
Freesound, credited in full on each work's page. The
[open call](https://omerkurtulus.com/what-the-corpus/) now running invites
recordings sent directly by contributors — instruments, objects, voice,
augmented and self-built instruments — for the corpus of the works to come.
Everyone who contributes is credited.

The call is open until 30 November 2026.
