*Strata* is an album in which I research the sonic archaeologies. Both pieces so far
excavate Istanbul, and both are dedicated to Halil İbrahim Tutar.

The two are the same excavation carried out by different means. *Strata* cuts the
trench by hand — a 36-channel periphonic field in which every layer sounds from the
first second, separated only by height. *STRATA II* hands the excavation to neural
models trained on nothing of the material they erode, and listens to what they
cannot carry.
