*Strata* is an album in which I research sound archaeologies: the layers a city
leaves behind in whatever can still be heard of it. Both pieces so far excavate
Istanbul, and both are dedicated to Halil İbrahim Tutar, with whom I walked this
city street by street.

The two are the same excavation carried out by different means. *Strata* cuts the
trench by hand — a 36-channel periphonic field in which every historical layer
sounds from the first second, separated only by height. *STRATA II* hands the
excavation to machines that have never heard the city, and listens to what they
cannot carry.
