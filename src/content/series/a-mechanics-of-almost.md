*A Mechanics of Almost* is a cycle of seven fixed-media electroacoustic works.
Each piece takes one source instrument and follows its mechanism — a membrane, a
reed, a bowed surface, a string under tension — to the point where the mechanism
itself is almost gone, and what remains is the residue of a physical gesture.

The seven works are *Anaculaton*, *Afterglare*, *Intergrain*, *Aerolith*,
*Craquelure*, *Endobody* and *Remanence*.

Released on Bandcamp.
