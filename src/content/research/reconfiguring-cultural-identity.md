Reconfiguring Cultural Identity in Contemporary Composition
 Contemporary classical and electroacoustic composers are increasingly engaging with cross-cultural elements to reconfigure cultural identity and instrumental relationships in their music. This research explores how composers reflect or reconstruct historical and cultural selfhood through sound, employing frameworks such as interculturality, inter-instrumentality, memory, post-folklorism, authenticity, and sound-texture conceptualization.
