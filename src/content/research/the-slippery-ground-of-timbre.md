Understanding timbre through a centralist mindset is nearly impossible; the concept
demands a multilayered approach that moves from branch to branch. Timbre is
routinely defined as everything beyond the known properties of sound — pitch, depth,
duration — a category understood largely through what it is not. That it has never
achieved a common definition, while remaining the most fundamental characteristic
determining a sound's identity, makes it a paradox in itself.

Written at Bowling Green State University, September 2025.
