Aerolith places an air-column instrument beside a resonating metal surface. The
bass clarinet is approached through breath, air tones, unstable low frequencies,
multiphonics and the changing resonances of a tube. The cymbal begins from another
condition: friction activates a rigid metallic surface whose partials may continue
long after the original gesture.

Fourth piece of *A Mechanics of Almost*.
