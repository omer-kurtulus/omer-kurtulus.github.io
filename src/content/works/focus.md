*Focus* reimagines and reinterprets ritual traditions from ancient Maya and
Egyptian civilisations, blending them with modern sensibilities to create a
hybridised sonic landscape. The work draws particularly on American Expressionist
poetry, opening a dialogue between ancient ceremonial practices and contemporary
artistic consciousness.

Written for sextet and fixed media, commissioned by FLYDLPHN Ensemble.
