*Lenition* unfolds as an alternation between two exciters that never quite
settle into one another: falling water, punctual and ametric, and bowed metal,
continuous and without attack.

Guided by continuous machine listening, Somax2 navigates and recombines a corpus
of bowed crotales, bowed cymbals and synthesized hammered strings without
reproducing its original sequence. Where the exciter is punctual, the system
tracks attack and pitch; where it turns continuous, no attacks remain and
navigation proceeds on timbre alone. The work moves between these two regimes of
listening rather than resolving them.

Individual fragments gradually lose their identity and become part of an
extended electroacoustic morphology: a form maintained through repetition,
deviation and continuous renewal.

The work was realised as a live human–machine performance. Its larger
trajectory, system parameters and interventions were shaped in real time by the
composer.
