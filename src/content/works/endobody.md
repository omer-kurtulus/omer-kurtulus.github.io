Endobody considers the body primarily as an arrangement of internal spaces. The
human voice is shaped not only by vocal-fold vibration but by cavities: throat,
mouth and changing vocal-tract geometry continuously reorganize its resonances. A
bass drum also consists of a membrane coupled to an enclosed resonating volume.
The work begins from this structural similarity.

Sixth piece of *A Mechanics of Almost*.
