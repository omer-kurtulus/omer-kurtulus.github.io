This piece for tenor saxophone draws on the Turkish musical form *uzun hava*
(*ora lunga*), known for its free rhythm and deep emotional expression. The
performance instructions aim to guide the player in capturing the intricate
dynamics and subtleties essential to the composition; the form and articulation
of the uzun hava are paralleled in the writing rather than quoted.
