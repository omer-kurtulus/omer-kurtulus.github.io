*False Return* approaches a heterogeneous vocal corpus as an unstable memory
whose responses repeatedly disappear and return in altered form.

A dry saxophone solo acts as the live exciter. Somax2 listens to its pitch,
articulation and spectral changes, using them to navigate and recombine a corpus
assembled from sung arpeggios, humming, sustained female voice, breathing,
whisper, breathy vowels and chopped vocal fragments.

Machine responses are withheld, fragmented and re-entered, allowing silence and
interruption to function as active formal boundaries. The work was realised as a
live human–machine performance; extended inactive intervals were shortened
afterward, while smaller gaps and abrupt returns were preserved as part of the
form.
