Remanence is concerned with what remains after the apparent cause of a sound has
disappeared. Prepared-piano strings, internal plucks, muted attacks, soundboard
resonances and mechanical sounds provide short events with complex internal decay.
Metal springs and coils introduce another form of stored energy: excitation can
travel, remain suspended and re-emerge after the initial gesture.

Seventh piece of *A Mechanics of Almost*.
