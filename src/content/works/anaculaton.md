Anaculaton begins with the timpani as an extremely simple causal event: impact →
membrane → resonance → decay. The piece gradually interferes with this sequence.
Timpani attacks activate virtual resonating bodies whose scale exceeds the apparent
dimensions of the original instrument. Field recordings — wind, ventilation,
tunnels, machinery, distant environmental resonance and other sustained spaces —
carry the sound beyond the instrument.

First piece of *A Mechanics of Almost*.
