A microscopic exploration of sonic archaeology and absolute measurement.
Constructed from raw data streams, pure sine waves and algorithmic micro-rhythms,
the piece navigates the threshold between sterile digital perfection and the
fragility of zero-error states. By processing fragmented acoustic artifacts
through strict generative DSP structures, it exposes the hidden, rigid
architecture of binary logic.

Inspired by the exactitude of atomic timekeeping and the absolute limits of
digital thresholds, *P(re)cise* observes the microscopic tension in data
transmission — measuring the infinite, cold space between zero and one.
