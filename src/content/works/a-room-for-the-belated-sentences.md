*A Room For The Belated Sentences* is written for TAK Ensemble, pre-recorded
corpus material and live electronics. The electronics were operated by the
composer. The work was made during the KEAR Electroacoustic Residency at Bowling
Green State University.

The ensemble plays against an archive of its own recorded material. SoMax 2 and
FluCoMa navigate that corpus in response to what is being played in the room, so
the pre-recorded layer answers the live one rather than accompanying it — a
sentence arriving late, after the moment that would have made it an answer.

## Documentation note

Two recordings are available. The *acoustic documentation* presents the acoustic
layer of the piece as performed in the room; *Version 1* is a further edit. A
recording of the full electroacoustic setting is not yet published.
