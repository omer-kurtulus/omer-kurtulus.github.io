Intergrain examines what appears to be a continuous scraping gesture at a much
smaller scale. A pick dragged across the wound surface of a guitar or bass string
crosses microscopic ridges: contact → interval → contact → interval. The güiro
presents a related structure at another physical scale: a continuous gesture
generated through successive collisions with a ridged surface.

Third piece of *A Mechanics of Almost*.
