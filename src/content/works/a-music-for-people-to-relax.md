This composition for live electronics explores the paradox of relaxation in urban
public spaces. It was created for Ensemble Ipse's *Flash Mass* event, in which up
to fifty composers' works are presented simultaneously through Bluetooth speakers
as a walking sound installation through Central Park.

The piece examines the ironic tension between parks as designated sites of rest
and the conditions that actually surround them: the city that never stops, the
crowd, the overlapping sound of forty-nine other pieces. Relaxation here is not a
state the music provides but one it keeps failing to arrive at.
