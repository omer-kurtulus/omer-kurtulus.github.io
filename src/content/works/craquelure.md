Craquelure brings together two forms of stored energy. A harp string is held under
tension until a pluck releases its energy into vibration. A ceramic or porcelain
surface appears stable until impact, stress or fracture reveals a network of
discontinuities. The work searches for a region in which these behaviours become
interchangeable.

Fifth piece of *A Mechanics of Almost*.
